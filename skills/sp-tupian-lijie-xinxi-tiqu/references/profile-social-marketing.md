# Profile: social-marketing

Turn covers, backgrounds, and B-roll candidates into searchable visual metadata.

## Core fields

- subjects and visible quantity: people, animals, products, food, scenery, buildings, interfaces, or text
- people count and visible presentation style, without identity or sensitive-attribute inference
- scene and visible environment
- mood and brightness (`bright`, `balanced`, `dark`)
- 2–5 dominant colors
- composition, focal point, subject position, whitespace, and shot scale
- existing text zones and safe title areas
- cover/background/body-image/B-roll suitability
- safe crop guidance for 1:1, 4:5, 9:16, and 16:9

## Safety

Appearance labels describe visible styling, clothing, pose, makeup, and commercial presentation only. Never infer race, nationality, religion, politics, health, disability, sexual orientation, personality, income, or real identity. Describe children only through scene and composition; never adultify them.

Use minimal P0 for images without marketing reuse value. Do not invent camera gear, brands, locations, or subjective quality scores.
