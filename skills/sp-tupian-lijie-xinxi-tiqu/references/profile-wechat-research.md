# Profile: wechat-research

Recover knowledge in public-article images that body text does not fully express.

## Extraction order

1. Screenshot: interface/product name, visible state, key operation, error, and conclusion text.
2. Chart: title, type, axes, series, legend, units, time scope, visible values, and trend.
3. Table image: headers, rows, columns, merged relationships, units, and notes; unreadable cells are `[unclear]`.
4. Flowchart: nodes, arrow direction, branch conditions, groups, start, and end.
5. Infographic: claims, hierarchy, comparisons, numbers, and visible source labels.
6. Photo/illustration: only subjects and facts relevant to the article argument.

## Keep or filter

Prefer images with claims, data, processes, product interfaces, case evidence, sources, or comparisons. Usually filter pure dividers, blank placeholders, avatars, follow prompts, generic tail cards, unrelated QR codes, and decorative promotions unless they are the research object.

## Accuracy

- Context may explain a label but cannot substitute for visible evidence.
- If exact values are unreadable, report trends only.
- Record missing source, unit, and time scope.
- Preserve separate text and image evidence even when they appear consistent.
